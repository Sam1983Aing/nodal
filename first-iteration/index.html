01 — The object

Fitted around the glass, not drawn around it.

The barrel is not a model. It walks the elements, takes the widest semi-diameter at each station along the axis and wraps a wall around whatever it finds. The follow-focus teeth are counted from the circumference at 0.8 module. Change one radius in the prescription and the tube reshapes itself to contain the result.

    04 — The trace

    Every bend is Snell's law, solved at the surface it happens on.

    Three bundles enter: one down the axis, one at two thirds of the field, one at the corner. Each ray is aimed to pass through the edge of the stop, intersected with the sphere of every surface it meets, and refracted there.

    The plane they meet was not placed by hand either. It was found by searching for the depth where the whole fan's spread is smallest.
